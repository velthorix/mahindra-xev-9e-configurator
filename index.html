About this study

An electric coupé,
built from code.

A real-time sculpture of the Mahindra XEV 9e — every panel, reflection and light signature authored procedurally in Three.js. No photographs, no imported model, no Blender.

Length4,789 mm
Width1,907 mm
Height1,694 mm
Wheelbase2,775 mm
Battery79 kWh
Range656 km MIDC

The electric SUV coupé blends a fast roofline with the presence of an SUV. Its closed grille, full-width front light bar and twin-peak emblem are recreated alongside a triple-screen cabin, 19-inch aero wheels and a flat battery floor. Use the view rail to walk around it, open the cabin, or look beneath the skin.
